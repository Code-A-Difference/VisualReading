SnapReader tries several curve strengths. For an arched line, it checks both curve directions. Text hidden around the back of an object cannot be recovered.

How to use SnapReader
  1. Point the camera at the words and hold steady. Use magnification or select only the text area for small print. Slight camera tilt is corrected automatically.
  2. Capture the image, then choose Recognize text. You can also choose a picture.
  3. After the scan, tap outlined lines to include or skip them. Keyboard users can tab to a line and press Enter or Space. Choose Read selected text to read only the lines you kept.
  4. For a ball, bottle, or arched line, open More image controls, enable Flatten curved print, and choose the closest shape. SnapReader compares several curve strengths.
  5. Review the result and any alternative readings, correct mistakes, then choose Read aloud. Use Reading and display settings to change contrast, text size, voice, and speaking speed.

Your picture stays in this browser. The first scan needs internet to download the English reading files.
