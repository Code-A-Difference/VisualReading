How to use SnapReader
  1. Point the camera at the words and hold steady, or choose a picture. Capture the image to freeze the view.
  2. The dotted yellow box appears on the captured picture. Drag its center to move it, or use the corner handles to resize it around the text you want. Keyboard users can focus the box and press an arrow key to move it, or focus a corner handle and press an arrow key to resize it. Hold Shift for larger steps.
  3. Choose Recognize text. With Text area only: on, only the box is sent to the shared AI service. Turn it off to recognize the whole picture.
  4. Review and correct the text, then choose Read aloud. You can change contrast, text size, voice, and speaking speed in Reading and display settings.
