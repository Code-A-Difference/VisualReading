How to use it

  • Point the camera at some words and press Read.
  • The words light up on the picture and are read to you. Tap a word to start from there.
  • Zoom with + and −, or pinch. Look changes the contrast.
  • Describe shows what an AI sees in the picture, ready as soon as you take it (switch it on in Settings).

Words are read on your device. Only if you switch on picture descriptions is the picture sent to a third-party AI provider.

Reading the words happens on your device with Tesseract.js: nothing is uploaded for that. Pictures are only sent anywhere (to a third-party AI provider) if you switch on “Describe every picture”.

Before the AI sees your pictures

Your pictures leave this device. When this is on, every picture you take is sent to the Code A Difference AI service, which passes it to a third-party AI provider to be described. That provider processes the picture under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, not ours. We don’t keep your pictures, but we can’t see, change or delete what the provider keeps.

Which provider answers can change from day to day. The ones used are Google (Gemini), OpenAI, Anthropic and similar services. Read their terms:

Google (privacy), OpenAI (privacy), Anthropic (privacy).

  • Don’t take pictures of private papers, IDs, health or money details, or of people who haven’t agreed.
  • The AI can be wrong. Don’t rely on it where a mistake would hurt.
  • You can switch this off any time in Settings. Reading the words never uses the AI: that happens on your device.
